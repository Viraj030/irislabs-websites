1bEye of Horus — three constructions, pick one
01 / IDENTITY

One skeleton, three languages

All three are drawn on the same 8px grid from the same brow / almond / teardrop / tail skeleton. Vector-native, single-weight gold.
ACircuit
PCB traces at 45°, terminating in solder pads. Reads as engineering on sight; the pads give it a rhythm no other direction has.
24×24 favicon test
BWireframe
Triangulated facets with an offset contour. The most "volumetric" of the three and the natural still frame for the hero, but the thinnest strokes are the first to fail at 24px.
24×24 favicon test
CScanline
Raster lines only, doubling in density across the eye band. Strongest cyberpunk read and the only one that carries texture — but it needs a solid-silhouette variant for small sizes.
